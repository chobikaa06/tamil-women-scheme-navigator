# தோழி (Thozhi) - Tamil Voice Guide to Government Schemes

An AI voice/text assistant for first-time women users in rural Tamil Nadu. No English, no typing, no prior digital knowledge needed: tap the big mic, speak in Tamil, hear the answer in Tamil.

## Problem
Millions of women are locked out of digital systems by design. Thozhi guides her to the right government scheme and tells her exactly where to apply.

## Features
- One big mic button, Tamil speech in and out (Web Speech API)
- Picture tiles for common needs (LPG, pregnancy aid, girl savings, skills, ration card)
- Gemini-powered answers: simple Tamil, one step at a time
- Never guesses amounts or dates; sends her to the nearest office
- Scales to more schemes by extending the system prompt

## Tech stack
HTML/CSS/JS, Vercel serverless function, Google Gemini API

## Run
Deploy on Vercel and set environment variable `GEMINI_API_KEY`.
